# Design Direction — Edward Artiaga Resume

Very light editorial portfolio with ink navy, parchment, and copper accents; structured like a calm operations brief with decisive hierarchy.

## Chosen Direction: Editorial Command Center

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial design inspired by independent magazines, Swiss information design, and premium operations reports.

### Core Principles
1. Make expertise feel calm, legible, and earned rather than loud.
2. Use asymmetric composition and strong typographic contrast to guide attention.
3. Pair tactile paper-like surfaces with precise operational details.
4. Treat every interaction as a small confirmation of competence.

### Color Philosophy
Deep ink navy anchors trust and seriousness. Warm parchment prevents the page from feeling corporate or sterile. Copper and ochre highlight moments of escalation, progress, and strategic thinking without resorting to generic tech blue.

### Layout Paradigm
A split editorial canvas: a narrow fixed-feeling rail for identity and navigation, paired with a wide reading column for evidence. Sections alternate between dense operational detail and generous breathing room, avoiding a centered marketing-card grid.

### Signature Elements
1. Copper rule lines and numbered section markers.
2. Oversized serif display headlines paired with compact sans-serif metadata.
3. Small “signal” labels that feel like status annotations from a support operations brief.

### Interaction Philosophy
Buttons should feel like clear controls: direct labels, visible state changes, and no mystery. Navigation scrolls to real sections, contact actions open the appropriate system surface, and the print action creates a practical resume output.

### Animation
Use short, snappy reveals with a slight upward drift, staggered by section. Hover states move borders and underlines rather than adding gratuitous glow. Respect reduced motion and avoid animating layout dimensions.

### Typography System
Display: Cormorant Garamond, used for name, section titles, and high-emotion statements. Body/UI: DM Sans, used for navigation, metadata, paragraphs, buttons, and competency labels. Use large serif scale for confidence, compact uppercase sans for operational clarity.

### Brand Essence
A calm escalation specialist who turns difficult customer moments into clear next steps. Personality: composed, strategic, dependable.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are concise and assured. CTAs are practical and specific. Microcopy sounds like an experienced teammate: direct, warm, and never inflated.
Example lines: “Make the complicated call feel simple.” / “Open a conversation about the next escalation.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A compact geometric mark combining a speech bubble, upward arrow, and chess-knight silhouette. Use the symbol as the recurring visual stamp; the text wordmark stays in typographic lockup beside it.

### Signature Brand Color
Copper Ochre — #C7794A. It signals action, judgment, and human warmth against the ink-and-parchment foundation.
